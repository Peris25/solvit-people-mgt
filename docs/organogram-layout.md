# Employee organogram

The Organogram page contains one custom SVG employee diagram. The previous static role-reference renderer and nested-list renderer have been removed. People and reporting edges come from the existing authorized organogram API. The user-confirmed governance presentation places the unique Managing Director and Executive Director side by side, joined by a dashed executive-peer link rather than an Executive Director-to-MD reporting line. This is a chart presentation rule, not a rewrite of stored manager assignments or approval routing. A Board of Directors box is drawn only when an explicit, resolved board reporting relationship is supplied. Unresolved roots stay separate.

The executive tier sits above a shared management row. Nested managers such as Timothy align with the other managers while retaining their existing parent connection, including Grace → Timothy. Same-row managerial reporting edges have directional arrows. Staff use compact cards and separate reporting rails; stacked siblings remain connected to their actual manager rather than to each other. The default fits the available page width, without horizontal scrolling or enlargement of small teams. Managers can expand/collapse their branch, including managers positioned on the same row. Search, team focus, whole-company reset, zoom, fullscreen and full-name/title details are available without another API request.

Validation includes real-parent edge checks, card non-overlap and canvas bounds, explicit board links, search, focus, keyboard interaction and fit controls. The SVG was rendered and visually inspected with a synthetic 23-person hierarchy; synthetic staff are not stored or used in production.
